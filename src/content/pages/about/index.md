## The theme

A quiet editorial blog theme for Astro and Tailwind CSS. It borrows the rhythm
of a newsroom — a wide lead story, a grid of smaller tiles, a narrow measure
for long-form reading — without copying anyone's assets or typefaces.

Everything visual is either open-source or generated: Inter and Inter Tight
under the SIL Open Font License, and abstract artwork drawn procedurally from
a hash of each post's title.

## What is configurable

Almost everything. `src/lib/site.ts` holds the site name, description, nav,
social links, footer text, pagination sizes, and the switches for each home
page section. The colour tokens live in one `@theme` block in
`src/styles/global.css`.
