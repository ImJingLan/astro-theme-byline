## A quiet editorial layout

This theme borrows the rhythm of a newsroom: a wide lead story, a grid of
smaller tiles underneath, and a narrow measure for long-form reading. It is
built with Astro and Tailwind CSS, and every colour lives in one `@theme`
block so the whole site can be re-skinned from a single file.

Nothing here is a screenshot of somewhere else. The type is Inter, the artwork
is generated, and the layout is an original arrangement of the same
typographic ideas.

## What you get

- A home page with a lead tile, a gallery carousel, and a list of recent posts
- An archive grouped by month, paginated
- Tag and category indexes generated from frontmatter
- Light and dark themes, with the choice remembered
- Page transitions that swap only the content column

## What you have to bring

Your posts, your domain, and your own taste in the config file. Everything in
`src/lib/site.ts` is a demonstration value.
