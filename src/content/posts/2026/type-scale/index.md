Long-form reading has different requirements from a landing page. A
landing page wants large type and short lines; an article wants a measure you
can follow for ten minutes without losing your place.

## The measure

The comfortable range for a single column of text is roughly 45 to 75
characters. This theme lands around 65 at the default width. Wider than that
and the eye has to travel too far back at the end of each line; narrower and
you spend more time moving between lines than reading them.

## Line height

Chinese and Latin text want different treatment. Latin text at 17px is usually
set with a line height near 1.6; Chinese benefits from a little more, because
the characters are denser and the eye needs clearer separation between rows.
This theme uses 1.8 for the body.

## Colour

Only one accent colour is used for links, and it is a blue dark enough to pass
contrast checks against both the white canvas and the grey section
backgrounds. Everything else is a neutral, which keeps the accent meaningful.
