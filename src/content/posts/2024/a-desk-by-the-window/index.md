The desk faces a window, which is worse for the screen and better for
everything else. There is a tree outside that changes colour slowly enough
that you only notice when you look up.

I have moved this desk four times. Each time the room got smaller and the
window got closer, which I am told is the wrong direction to move in.

## What actually matters

A surface at the right height, a chair that does not make you think about it,
and light that is not directly behind the screen. Everything else is
decoration.
