Most of what I know came from someone else writing it down badly
first. A half-finished post with the right idea in it is more useful than a
polished one that never gets published.

The trick is to write the version you would have wanted to find. Not the
authoritative treatment — the one that assumes you are tired, it is late, and
you need the answer to one specific question.

## Keep the failure

When something does not work, that is usually the interesting part. The
successful path is often the one already in the documentation. The three hours
lost to a mismatched version number is the part nobody else wrote down.
