Every blog starts with a post like this one. It exists mostly to prove
the pipeline works: the frontmatter parses, the date sorts correctly, and the
archive has something to show.

The plan is to write about what I am building, what I am reading, and the
occasional thing that took longer than it should have. Short posts are fine.
Unfinished posts are fine. The point is to keep the habit.
