Lumen Reader is now available on Android. The app keeps the same reading surface as the desktop and iOS versions — 17px body text, generous line height, and a single column that never widens past 68 characters.

## What is different on Android

Android users expect different things from an app, so a straight port would have been the wrong decision. Three things changed:

- **System text scaling is respected everywhere**, including inside long articles, without breaking the column width.
- **The share sheet is native**, so saved articles can go straight into any app that accepts a link.
- **Back navigation follows platform convention**, with predictive back gestures supported on Android 14 and later.

## Reading position syncs

If you start an article on your phone and finish it on a laptop, the position is preserved to the paragraph. This works offline too: positions are recorded locally and reconciled the next time the device reaches the network.

## Availability

Lumen Reader for Android is free, requires Android 10 or later, and is available today. A Lumen account is optional — the app works as a standalone reader without one.
